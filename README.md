\# 🧠 MEMORA



\### Don't Let Important Things Get Lost.



MEMORA is a personal AI memory assistant that helps you capture thoughts, tasks, events, ideas, and useful personal information.



Instead of trying to remember everything yourself, simply tell MEMORA what is on your mind. MEMORA uses AI to understand the information and organize it into:



\- ✅ Tasks

\- 📅 Events

\- 🧠 Memories



You can then view your saved information or ask MEMORA questions about it.



\---



\## ✨ Why MEMORA?



People often have important thoughts throughout the day:



> "I need to submit my assignment tomorrow."



> "I should call Rahul tonight."



> "My favorite subject is BCE."



> "I have an idea for my next project."



These thoughts are easy to forget.



MEMORA turns these unorganized thoughts into structured information that can be saved and retrieved later.
